Why Sarva Samrudhhi? సర్వ సమృద్ధి ఎందుకు?

A Platform Built Upon Grassroots Dignity and Accountability

Sarva Samrudhhi Independent Party was established to provide the citizens of Deverakonda Constituency with an uncompromised, people-centric political choice. The name 'Sarva Samrudhhi' embodies our core belief: that development must be holistic, reaching every farmer, family, student, and worker in our villages and towns without bias or neglect.

Our Stated Commitment:
Direct constituent participation in policy decisions, transparent allocation of community resources, and vigilant defense of local civic interests.

Core Policy Pillars

Stated Objectives & Development Priorities

The party works systematically across verified socio-economic focus areas essential for the sustained prosperity of Deverakonda.

Agriculture & Water Security

Strengthening local canal irrigation, ensuring reliable power supply, seed security, and fair market procurement for small and tenant farmers.

Roads & Rural Infrastructure

Connecting interior hamlets and thandas to primary mandal roads, improving bridges, drainage, and reliable public transit.

Quality Public Healthcare

Modernizing primary health centres (PHCs), round-the-clock emergency medical transit, and accessible specialty care in Deverakonda.

Education & Youth Skills

Upgrading government schools, digital literacy laboratories, residential hostel amenities, and career skill training for local youth.

Livelihood & Employment

Promoting local agro-processing hubs, cottage industries, vocational training, and transparent employment guidance for jobseekers.

Women & Family Welfare

Supporting self-help groups (SHGs), maternity health infrastructure, women safety patrols, and nutritional support programs.

Safe Drinking Water

Ensuring clean, fluoride-safe drinking water supply to every household and rejuvenating traditional village percolation tanks.

Transparent Public Governance

Regular open public grievance assemblies (Grama Vedikas), ward-level participatory budgeting, and anti-corruption oversight.

Institutional Methodology

How We Work

A disciplined, step-by-step participatory process that ensures every community voice translates into accountable public action.

Phase 01

01. Listen

Conducting continuous village listening tours to understand unaddressed local grievances.

Phase 02

02. Understand

Reviewing technical data, revenue records, and administrative bottlenecks with local residents.

Phase 03

03. Discuss

Convening open community consultations to debate viable solutions democratically.

Phase 04

04. Organize

Forming transparent task teams composed of local elders, youth, and subject professionals.

Phase 05

05. Act

Constructive administrative representations, legal petitions, and public advocacy.

Phase 06

06. Report

Publishing periodic public scorecards on action taken and progress achieved.
